DentaVive ("DentaVive," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit dntavive.com (the "Site") or purchase our products, how we use and share that information, and the choices available to you. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

  • Information you provide directly: your name, email address, shipping address, billing address, and phone number when you place an order, subscribe to updates, or contact customer support.
  • Order and transaction information: the products you purchase, order history, and related correspondence. Card numbers themselves are processed by our third-party payment processor and are not stored on our servers (see Section 5).
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring URLs, and general location data, collected through cookies and similar technologies when you browse the Site.
  • Communications: messages you send us through contact forms, email, or customer support channels.

Account & Order Info

Name, shipping/billing address, email, phone number

Technical Info

IP address, browser, device, approximate location

Communications

Support emails, chat messages, form submissions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders, including shipping and order confirmations
  • Provide customer support and respond to your questions or requests
  • Send order updates, receipts, and, if you've opted in, marketing emails about DentaVive products and promotions
  • Improve the Site, our products, and the overall shopping experience
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other unlawful activity
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations, such as tax and accounting requirements
Marketing emails are optional. Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time without affecting your ability to receive order-related communications.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Like most websites, we use cookies and similar technologies (such as pixels and local storage) to keep the Site functioning properly, remember your cart, understand how visitors use the Site, and measure the performance of our marketing campaigns, including affiliate and advertising links.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, though doing so may affect how parts of the Site function, such as your shopping cart.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the business — such as payment processors, shipping and fulfillment partners, email service providers, and website hosting or analytics providers — under agreements that limit their use of your data to providing services to us.
  • Affiliate and advertising partners, in aggregated or de-identified form, to measure the performance of referral links and ad campaigns.
  • Legal and safety purposes, where we believe disclosure is required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of DentaVive, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers, such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case customer information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. Payment & Order Data

Payments made on the Site are processed by a PCI-compliant third-party payment processor. We do not store your full card number on our servers. The payment processor's own privacy policy governs how they handle your payment details.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including to process your orders, comply with legal and tax obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain uses such as marketing communications or, where applicable, the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 11. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law and may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site and our products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through the support information provided on your order confirmation, or via the contact details listed on the Site.
