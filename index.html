What Is DentaVive?

Most dental products work on the surface. A toothpaste cleans, a mouthwash rinses, and then their effect is gone. DentaVive takes a different approach: instead of a topical fix, it's formulated as an internal support system for the bacteria and minerals your mouth relies on every day.

Each daily capsule combines five probiotic strains — Lactobacillus reuteri, Lactobacillus paracasei, Bifidobacterium lactis BL-04, Lactobacillus salivarius, and Streptococcus salivarius K12 — with tricalcium phosphate, the same mineral compound that makes up tooth enamel, and inulin, a natural prebiotic fiber that helps those probiotic strains establish themselves.

The core idea behind DentaVive is simple: a healthy mouth depends on a healthy balance of bacteria, not the complete elimination of it. Alcohol-based rinses can wipe out the bacteria in your mouth indiscriminately. DentaVive is built around restoring balance instead.

DentaVive is a dietary supplement intended to complement daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups — it is not a replacement for professional dental care.

The Oral Microbiome

Balanced vs. Imbalanced Oral Bacteria

Your mouth is home to billions of bacteria. Whether that community works for you or against you often comes down to balance.

Balanced Oral Microbiome

Diverse bacterial population that helps crowd out odor- and decay-associated bacteria.

Steady saliva pH that supports a less acidic environment for enamel.

Calmer gum tissue with less day-to-day irritation.

Fresher breath from fewer odor-causing compounds.

Imbalanced Oral Microbiome

Overgrowth of acid-producing bacteria that can outcompete beneficial strains.

More acidic environment, which can leave enamel more vulnerable over time.

More irritated gum tissue that can feel tender or sensitive.

Persistent odor that mints and rinses only mask temporarily.
